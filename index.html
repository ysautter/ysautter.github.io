Hey, thanks for stopping by!

My name is Yannick Sautter.
I lead the development of groupios, a secure multi-tenant e-mail and groupware platform, at step3IT. Besides building the product, I take care of the infrastructure it runs on, how we monitor and secure it, and the development process around it.

During my studies I focused on Cybersecurity and Machine Learning. Some of that research has been published at ARES and ESORICS. I also love tinkering with software, especially to improve my own Linux workflow.

Publications

  • ESORICS 2026
    Internationalizing DGA Detection
    Arthur Drichel, Yannick Sautter, Ulrike Meyer
    European Symposium on Research in Computer Security, Rome · LNCS 16968, pp. 145–165

    We show that internationalized domain names let DGAs evade state-of-the-art classifiers about 98% of the time, and we propose a language-balanced training procedure to counter this.

    DOI
  • ARES 2026
    DomainBERT: Transformer-based Domain Generation Algorithm Detection
    Arthur Drichel, Yannick Sautter, Ulrike Meyer
    International Conference on Availability, Reliability and Security, Linköping · short paper

    A transformer-based DGA classifier that outperforms the state of the art in a bias-reduced setting: +11.23% ROC AUC for FPR ≤ 0.01 and +13.98% TPR at an FPR of 0.0014.

    Program
  • arXiv 2020
    BAAAN: Backdoor Attacks Against Autoencoder and GAN-Based Machine Learning Models
    Ahmed Salem, Yannick Sautter, Michael Backes, Mathias Humbert, Yang Zhang
    Preprint, based on my bachelor thesis

    Extends backdoor attacks from classifiers to autoencoders and GANs. A hidden trigger makes the model produce attacker-chosen output while it keeps behaving normally otherwise.

    arXiv
