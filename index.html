My name is Yannick Sautter.
I lead the development of groupios, a secure multi-tenant e-mail and groupware platform, at step3IT. Besides building the product, I take care of the infrastructure it runs on, how we monitor and secure it, and the development process around it.

I also love tinkering with software, especially to improve my own Linux workflow. Since data privacy matters a lot to me, I also run my own LLMs locally for anything private.

Self-Hosted Server & Home Lab

One VPS is the only way in. Everything else lives at home, behind WireGuard.

Swipe to see the whole diagram →

How a photo from my phone gets backed up

  1. My phone uploads it to media.sautter.dev.
  2. sniproxy routes by SNI. Mail goes straight to Stalwart.
  3. nginx-proxy terminates TLS with Let's Encrypt.
  4. The Tailscale gateway forwards it over WireGuard.
  5. Immich runs at home as its own tailnet node.
  6. restic backs it up every night and verifies the repository.
  • Headscale is my own Tailscale control plane, with no third-party account
  • Every home app gets its own Tailscale sidecar
  • fail2ban with my own filters; I review the logs for attacks that slipped through
  • Pi-hole with DNS-over-HTTPS for all my devices

Publications

  • ESORICS 2026
    Internationalizing DGA Detection
    Arthur Drichel, Yannick Sautter, Ulrike Meyer
    European Symposium on Research in Computer Security, Rome · LNCS 16968, pp. 145–165 · result of my master thesis

    We show that internationalized domain names let DGAs evade state-of-the-art classifiers about 98% of the time, and we propose a language-balanced training procedure to counter this.

    DOI Code
  • ARES 2026
    DomainBERT: Transformer-based Domain Generation Algorithm Detection
    Arthur Drichel, Yannick Sautter, Ulrike Meyer
    International Conference on Availability, Reliability and Security, Linköping · short paper · result of my master thesis

    A transformer-based DGA classifier that outperforms the state of the art in a bias-reduced setting: +11.23% ROC AUC for FPR ≤ 0.01 and +13.98% TPR at an FPR of 0.0014.

    DOI Code
  • arXiv 2020
    BAAAN: Backdoor Attacks Against Autoencoder and GAN-Based Machine Learning Models
    Ahmed Salem, Yannick Sautter, Michael Backes, Mathias Humbert, Yang Zhang
    Preprint, based on my bachelor thesis

    Extends backdoor attacks from classifiers to autoencoders and GANs. A hidden trigger makes the model produce attacker-chosen output while it keeps behaving normally otherwise.
